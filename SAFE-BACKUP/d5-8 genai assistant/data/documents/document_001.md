# Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

## Purpose

This document provides an overview of Artificial Intelligence and explains how AI systems are used to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

## Requirements

AI systems generally require data, algorithms, computational resources, and a suitable problem definition.

## Process

An AI system processes available data, applies predefined algorithms or learned patterns, and produces an output based on the input.

## Applications

Artificial Intelligence is commonly used in recommendation systems, virtual assistants, image recognition, fraud detection, and automated decision-support systems.

## Responsibilities

Teams working with AI systems should ensure that the system is properly tested, documented, and used for its intended purpose.
