# Computer Vision

## Purpose

This document describes Computer Vision and explains how AI systems analyze and interpret images and videos.

## Requirements

Computer Vision applications require image or video data, preprocessing methods, suitable models, and computational resources.

## Process

Input images are first prepared through preprocessing operations. A trained model then identifies patterns, objects, features, or other visual information.

## Applications

Computer Vision is used in object detection, facial recognition, medical image analysis, quality inspection, autonomous systems, and image classification.

## Responsibilities

Teams should verify image quality, evaluate model accuracy, and consider variations in lighting, camera position, image resolution, and other environmental factors.
